📖 How to use this page

Your daily routine

  1. Morning coffee. Open the page and look at the signal chip at the top.
  2. Chip grey? No lull right now — close the tab and check back later.
  3. Chip green or blue? Good conditions. If you like the look of the chart, click BUY.
  4. Three lines appear on the chart. Now it's just keeping an eye on it — like the old days.
  5. Price reaches the green TARGET line → click SELL. Cycle complete. ✔
  6. Price drops to the red STOP line → click SELL too. That's the rule that keeps you safe.
  7. Two cycles maximum, then walk away — the same discipline that worked for you on Binance.

The signal chip

  • Green — BUY WINDOW: the last 30 minutes are quieter than 80% of the 24-hour average. The market is calm — your classic lull.
  • Blue — lull + RSI dip: same lull, and RSI under 30. The strongest setup this page can show you.
  • Grey: the market is too jumpy. Patience — a lull always comes.

The chip shows the numbers in brackets, e.g. "range 157% of avg" — below 100% means quieter than usual, below 80% triggers the green light.

The three lines after you buy

  • Amber — BOUGHT: your entry price.
  • Green — TARGET: entry + 0.7%. Selling here keeps you +0.5% after fees. The "Distance to target" number counts down as price gets close.
  • Red — STOP: entry − 1%. If price touches it, sell immediately. Losses on this strategy grind down slowly — they don't bounce back in time.

The buttons

  • BUY — puts all your USDT into BTC at the current live price. Real Bybit fees apply (~0.1%).
  • SELL — sells it all back to USDT (another real ~0.1% fee) and records the cycle.
  • reset session — clears the stats (cycles, P&L) only. Your real balances never change. Asks before it does.

Your session is saved automatically on the server — close the tab, switch MacBooks, it is always the same session. Note: the buttons need you to be logged into Agent Zero on that browser.

The session panel

  • Equity — what your Bybit account is worth right now (live balances).
  • Net vs start — your total profit or loss since the last reset.
  • Cycles — completed buy→sell round trips.
  • Wins / Losses — how many cycles ended up vs down.
  • Fees paid — real fees paid to Bybit. Watch this: it adds up fast if you overtrade.

⭐ The golden rules

  1. Never buy without a lull. The green chip is your permission slip.
  2. Never move the stop. If the red line is hit, you sell. No "it'll come back".
  3. Two cycles a day, maximum. Then close the tab and live your life.
  4. This is real money now. Every click is final — size each trade so a loss is a lesson, not a wound.
